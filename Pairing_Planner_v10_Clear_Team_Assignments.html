Saturday Golf — Pairing Planner

Build suggested teams and tee-time groups from a Squabbit roster. Set team size, tee-time size, and whether teammates must stay together. Team assignments and tee-time groups are shown separately after you generate options.

1. Event setup

2. Players attending

Import the upcoming Squabbit CSV roster, or paste one player per line as Player Name, Handicap Index. Best Ball balance uses Handicap Index (not Playing Handicap). The CSV importer looks for player/name and handicap index/handicap columns; you can review and correct values before generating groups.

3. Generate team and tee-time options

After you click Generate best groupings, each option shows a dedicated Suggested Teams list followed by the Suggested Tee Times. Each golfer is labeled with their assigned team.

For Best Ball, each foursome is assessed by the sum of its three lowest Handicap Index values. The planner aims to keep those sums close across groups while avoiding repeat pairings. It does not treat this as a team-vs-team pairing rule.
