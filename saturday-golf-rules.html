League Rules

Local rules, format guidelines, and scoring procedures
🔍

Handicaps

For all games played during our matches, net handicaps are calculated from GHIN system. It's expected that each golfer will enter all scores from rounds played into GHIN system weekly.

Tracking of Round Scores

Each foursome to turn in one scorecard at end of round. Both carts should keep score (on card) and compare after 9th hole and also compare after 18th hole. Scorecard will have:

  1. gross scores entered, for each player, for all 18 holes
  2. total of gross scores, for each player, for front 9, back 9 and 18.
  3. Confirmation that total gross scores on scorecard, for each player, match their total gross scores in Squabbit
  4. Match Play Only - Both teams/carts should track match score (+ or -) and confirm after each hole.

First Tee

Breakfast ball allowed on first tee only. Must play the second ball for remainder of hole

Fairways and Fringe

Lift, clean and place allowed when in fairway or fringe

Ball can be moved from divot or wet area in fairway or fringe

"Play the Ball Down" in Rough and Sand Traps

This means you can only move or touch your ball when in the fairway, on the green or on fringe

Putting

Gimmee = 2 feet (Except in Ryder Cup format) Putt must be "given" by a golfer in your group for all formats

Unplayable Ball (General Area)

Two Options for 1 penalty stroke

  1. Play another ball from where previous stroke was made
  2. Lateral relief (two club lengths) no closer to hole

Bunker - Unplayable Ball

Unplayable Ball in Bunker. Four options:

  1. Play another ball from where the previous stroke was made for 1 penalty stroke
  2. Take back on the line relief in the bunker for 1 penalty stroke
  3. Take lateral relief in the bunker (two club lengths) for 1 penalty stroke
  4. Take back-on-the-line relief outside the bunker for 2 penalty strokes

Lost Ball or Out of Bounds

If you think ball might be out bounds (past white stakes) or that you might not find it, you can play a provisional ball, If you are then unable to find your original ball, or you find it out of bounds, your provisional ball is your ball in play for 1 penalty stroke. You can also take relief under the local rule for 2 penalty strokes. Estimate the spot where ball came to rest on course or estimated spot where it crossed the out of bounds line. Take two club lengths from nearest spot on fairway (no closer to the hole). Does not apply if ball is lost in penalty area.

Penalty Areas

Yellow Stakes - Two Relief Options for 1 penalty stroke

  1. Play another ball from where previous stroke was made
  2. Drop ball outside penalty area keeping point where ball entered penalty area between the hole and point where the ball is dropped

Red Stakes - Three Relief Options for 1 penalty stroke

  1. Play another ball from where previous stroke was made
  2. Drop ball outside penalty area keeping point where ball entered penalty area between the hole and point where the ball is dropped
  3. Lateral relief (two club lengths from penalty area)

Abnormal Course Conditions (General Area)

Any of these four defined conditions: Animal Hole, Ground Under Repair, Immovable Obstructions or Temporary Water; Examples of an immovable obtruction are cart path, sprinkler head, metal drains. Fence posts and trees are NOT considered Immovable Obstruction

Meaning: 1) player's ball touches or is in abnormal course condition or 2) abnormal course condition physically interferes with player's area of intended stance or area of intended swing or 3) only when ball is on putting green, an abnormal course condition on or off the putting green intervenes on the line of play.

Free relief of 1 club length is allowed from the nearest point of complete relief When taking free relief, you must find the closest spot where you are no longer interfered with by the abnormal course condition, which can be on the fairway even if your ball was originally in the rough. Free relief cannot be taken when your ball lies in penalty area.

Other Local Rules

  • Playing Wrong Ball - If a player mistakenly hits the wrong ball, that player will not be assessed any penalty strokes
  • Bunkers - You are allowed to lift, rake and replace when bunkers have not previously been raked

Max Hole Scores

  • Double Bogey for Quota/Stableford Game
  • Net Triple Bogey for all other formats (except Ryder Cup)
